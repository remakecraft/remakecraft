# Code of Conduct

Remakecraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text there is the binding version. This page summarises it and says how it works here.

It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and anywhere you represent the project in public.

## The short version

**Do:** be kind and patient, respect different opinions and experience levels, give and take criticism of the work rather than the person, own your mistakes, and put the community ahead of winning an argument.

**Don't:** sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment in public or private, publishing someone's private information (an address, a real name they have not shared) without permission, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Many people here are students and first-time contributors. Treat a beginner's question as a question, not a nuisance.

## Project-specific lines

- **No targeting real people or companies.** Disagreeing with a vendor is fine. Harassing their staff, or using the project to do it, is not.
- **No requests to break the law.** Clone requests for DRM, anti-cheat or security software are refused (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Arguing for them repeatedly after a refusal counts as disruption.
- **Wall bypasses go to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)**, not to a public thread. Posting a working, unfixed bypass publicly is treated as a conduct issue.

## Reporting

Report to the maintainer privately:

- GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/remakecraft/remakecraft/security/advisories/new), with "Conduct" in the title.

Include links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are handled in confidence. You get a reply within 7 days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant's four steps, scaled to the harm:

| Step | For | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour | A private, written warning with an explanation |
| 2. Warning | A single serious incident or a pattern | A warning, plus no interaction with the people involved for a set time |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour | No interaction or posting in any project space for a set time |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people | Removed from all project spaces for good |
